Part B: Ethical Considerations & Risk Mitigation Protocol
Project ALVI — Autonomous Companion Robot (Jira: SCRUM-61 / ST-09.2)
Ref: CS-43-2026

Portfolio Chapter 5
1. Vulnerable Participant Safeguards (Elderly & Patients)
To protect elderly participants and care recipients from exploitation or privacy breaches, all interviews strictly enforce Option B (Mandatory Anonymity). Real patient names, residential addresses, and exact medical diagnoses are permanently withheld. Transcripts are coded under academic pseudonyms (e.g. [Subject: Patient-01]). Formal legal guardians or family caregivers are permitted to attend sessions upon request.
2. Informed Consent & Retrospective Withdrawal Protocol
All participants receive the official Participant Informed Consent & Recognition Form (Doc Ref: ALVI-ETH-01-V2) prior to engagement. Consent is explicit, written, and recorded with 6 affirmative checkpoints. Participants maintain the unqualified right to pause or terminate the session. A strict retrospective withdrawal cut-off date is enforced: participants may withdraw data within 7 calendar days post-interview or before 20 November 2026, after which findings are irreversibly synthesized into portfolio deliverables.
3. Data Security, Privacy & Destruction Policy (PDPA 2022 & UK GDPR)
Under Section 10 of the Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 and Article 5(1)(e) of UK GDPR: (a) All audio/video recordings and raw notes are stored in an AES-256 encrypted storage volume restricted by two-factor authentication to Team 43 leads. (b) Live vitals (heart rate BPM) and vision AI streams are processed locally in real-time on the Tier 3 edge laptop without remote storage. (c) Permanent Secure Destruction: All raw recordings will be permanently purged and shredded within 30 days of module exam board ratification (no later than 31 May 2027).
4. Hardware Safety & Prototyping Risk Assessment
The robotic mobile base operates strictly at safe low-voltage DC (12V SLA battery and 5V USB step-downs). Pill dispenser cylinders are fabricated from non-toxic, food-grade PLA filament. Hardware includes a physical emergency power kill-switch, ultrasonic and LiDAR collision stop buffers (minimum 25cm safety radius), and optical IR break-beam verification to avoid dispenser pinch risks.
