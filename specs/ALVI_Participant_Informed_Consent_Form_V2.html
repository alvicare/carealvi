Research Purpose, Governance & Participant Rights Statutory Ethics Disclosure

You are invited to participate in a 45-to-60 minute stakeholder requirements elicitation interview for the ALVI Autonomous Home-Care Robot (featuring 3-step AI pill intake confirmation, contactless vitals monitoring, 2D LiDAR navigation, dual-confirmed fall detection, and WebRTC video communication). The objective is to understand real-world eldercare workflows, safety protocols, and caregiver user experience needs.

Voluntary Participation & Right to Withdraw: Participation is entirely voluntary. You may pause the session, refuse to answer any question, or withdraw at any time without penalty or disadvantage. Retrospective Withdrawal Cut-Off Date: If you wish to withdraw your data retrospectively after the interview, you may notify the research team via email (amodhgunawardana@gmail.com / nethum.20250172@iit.ac.lk) within 7 calendar days of the interview or prior to 20 November 2026 (whichever is earlier). After this cut-off, interview findings will be irreversibly anonymized, aggregated, and synthesized into the published CW1 Portfolio submission.

Storage Limitation & Secure Destruction (PDPA §10 / UK GDPR Art 5(1)(e)): All recordings and transcripts will be stored in an access-restricted, AES-256 encrypted repository accessible strictly to the authorized Team 43 student researchers. All raw audio and video recordings will be permanently purged and securely shredded within 30 days of final CW2 examination board ratification (no later than 31 May 2027).
